FamTM (Family Tree) — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Family Tree app (FamTM) for Android, version 1.3 and later. Effective 4 November 2026.

The short version: your family tree, the notes you write about people, and their photos stay in the app's own storage on your phone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours — we cannot see your family, and neither can anyone else through us.

Birthday reminders are worked out and shown on the phone. The one thing the app fetches from outside is a newer copy of its own interface, from this project's GitHub repository; that request carries nothing from your tree.

Who we are

FamTM — shown on the phone as “Family Tree” — is an independent Android app. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the app's developer, and “the app” means FamTM. You can reach us at the address in Contact.

What the app keeps on your phone

The people in your tree, the relationships between them, the notes you write, and the pictures you add are stored as data inside the app's own private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and it is not uploaded anywhere. The pictures the app keeps are small copies — a photo is scaled down before it is stored — and that copy lives with the entry it belongs to.

The app does not read your contacts, your calendar, your messages, your files, your location or your accounts, and it has no permission to do any of those things. Everyone in the tree is typed in or imported by you.

Camera and photos

You can take a photo or pick one from the phone to add to a person. When you take one, the camera app is handed a one-off permission to write into the app's own private folder; the app itself declares no camera and no storage permission, and the picture is never added to your phone's gallery.

Only the most recent picture waiting to be added is kept: that folder is emptied the next time you take or pick a picture, and when the app starts. A picture you pick is read once, scaled down, and stored with the person it belongs to.

Nothing you photograph or pick is sent anywhere by the app.

Birthday reminders

If you turn reminders on, the app keeps the day and month of each living person's birthday so it can show you a reminder on the day. That check runs on your phone using Android's own background-work scheduler, and the reminder is a notification raised by your phone — the birthday itself is not sent anywhere. On Android 13 and later you are asked for notification permission only when you switch reminders on; if you decline, the rest of the app works unchanged.

Exports and backups you make

The app can write a PDF or a JSON backup. When you choose that, Android's own file dialog asks you where to put it and you pick the folder, so the copy goes exactly where you send it. That export is the one way your data leaves the app's storage — and it happens only because you asked for it.

What leaves your phone

Looking for a newer version of the interface

On launch, and when you tap the ⟳ button, the app asks this project's GitHub repository for a newer copy of its own interface page. Like any web request, that one carries your IP address and standard request headers, which GitHub receives and handles under GitHub's privacy statement. It carries no identifier for you or your phone, and nothing from your family tree — no names, notes, photos or birthdays.

If a newer page is found it is saved in the app's private storage and used the next time the app opens, so the interface can be corrected without waiting for a store update. An app update you install always wins over that downloaded copy, and if a downloaded page fails to load the app falls back to the interface built into the installed version.

There is no update check on any other schedule, and the app opens no other connection.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, transmit or store your family tree, photos or notes on any server. There is no server of ours.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or attribution software.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone, because we do not have it.
  • We do not require a name, email address, phone number or account of any kind.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use your tree or photos to train anything, and no automated system reads them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your phone, in its private storage.

  • To remove a person, a note or a photo, delete it in the app.
  • To remove everything at once, uninstall the app. Android deletes its private storage, including the tree, the photos and any downloaded interface copy.
  • To stop birthday reminders, switch them off in the app, and revoke notification permission in Android Settings if you want no notifications from the app at all.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request to send us.

Backups

FamTM allows Android's own app-backup feature. If backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the app's stored data — your tree, notes and the small photo copies — in your device backup or your Google account's backup. That copy is controlled by your Google settings and by Google's privacy policy, not by us. You can turn backup off in Android Settings, and uninstalling the app removes its data from the device.

Children

FamTM is a family-tree app for general use and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, of any age. It does store the names and birthdays you enter about other people, including children in your family, so treat that as you would any private record on your phone.

Changes to this policy
