Your family, with every birthday in it.

FamTM is a family tree for one phone: the people, the dates they were born and died, how old they are today, a portrait if you have one, and the notes worth keeping. No account, nothing uploaded.

One screen per person, and the facts that matter

Add a person once and the tree does the arithmetic — ages, who is oldest, whose birthday is next — with no spreadsheet behind it.

People and dates

Name, born, died, and the family they belong to. A date can be typed the way you say it — 4 Jul, 7/4/1986 or just 1986.

Ages that are right

An age is counted from the date, not the year, so someone born in December is 39 until December.

Two birthdays

One real birthday, and — if a passport carries a different one — a second date kept quietly beside it. Ages still count from the real one.

Portraits

A photo per person, taken or picked, kept with the entry instead of hunted for later.

Birthday reminders

Whose birthday is near, on the opening screen, so the day does not pass because nobody was looking.

Notes and exports

A free-text note for each person, and a backup you can write out as a file whenever you like.

Where it lives

Not in an account, not in a database somewhere — in the app's own storage on this phone.

Everything is local. People, dates, portraits and notes are written to the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. There is no sign-in, no sync and no server holding a copy.

The app updates itself. Its screen is a page the app keeps on this project's GitHub Pages address; it takes a newer copy when it starts, without an installer. That check asks for one file and carries nothing about your family.

Backups are yours to make. Export whenever you like and keep the file where you keep important things. Uninstalling takes the app's storage with it, so an export is how a tree moves to a new phone.

Privacy, in one paragraph

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking — and nothing about your family leaves the phone.

The camera is used only when you take a portrait, and the picture is saved with that person. Reminders are worked out on the phone. The only outside request is the check for a newer copy of the app's own screen, which carries no names, dates or photographs.

The whole statement, in the same plain words, is in the privacy policy.
