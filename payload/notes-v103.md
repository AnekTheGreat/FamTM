## 2026-10-14 — v1.0.3: The exported PDF shows the family tree

A page change: it arrives over the air, nothing to install. The page now carries its own version,
**v1.0.3**, and shows it in Settings.

### Changed
- **Export PDF now draws the tree.** The first page of the PDF is the family as it is really drawn —
  every box, every name, every date, every photo, the bar between a married couple and the lines down
  to their children. It is laid out by the same engine that draws the Map and measured the same way,
  so what you print is the tree you are looking at, with nothing folded away.
- **The page is turned whichever way round fits your family bigger.** A family that is wide is
  printed across the page, and a family that is tall down it, so the whole tree is one page and as
  large as it can be on that page.
- **The two lists are still there, after the picture.** First the tree as a drawing, then the
  descendant tree as an indented list, then everyone A–Z with their dates. Nothing the PDF had
  before has been taken out, and the pages are still numbered.
- **If the tree cannot be drawn the PDF is what it always was**: the two lists, and a line saying so
  instead of a blank page. A backup, a restore and everything else are untouched — this is the
  export only.
